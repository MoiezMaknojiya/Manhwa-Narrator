

# COMMENTARY LAYER (policy — YouTube "inauthentic/reused content" se bachne ke liye)

Ye script sirf retelling NAHI honi chahiye. YouTube aur publishers "pure recap" ko reused content maante hain; jo cheez isse alag karti hai wo narrator ki APNI raaye, analysis aur reactions hain. Is liye:

1. **Hook (pehle marker ke neeche, 2-3 jumle):** Pehle image marker ke baad sab se pehle narrator apne andaaz mein ek chhota hook de — is chapter mein kya bada hone wala hai, kyun dekhna chahiye. Spoiler nahi, excitement.
2. **Commentary har 2-3 images par (kam az kam):** Narration ke beech narrator apni taraf se ek short line bole — reaction ("yahan mujhe laga ke..."), analysis ("notice karo artist ne..."), theory ("mera guess hai ke..."), foreshadowing, ya character ki psychology par note. Ye lines us image ke section ke ANDAR hi rahen (marker se pehle nahi, baad mein), taake sync na toote. Inhe quotes/attribution format mein NAHI likhna — ye narrator khud bol raha hai, seedha flowing paragraph mein.
3. **Dialogue kabhi word-for-word nahi:** Bubble ka matlab apne alfaaz mein do; agar koi line iconic hai toh usko paraphrase karo, copy nahi.
4. **Closing analysis (aakhri section mein, like/subscribe line se PEHLE, 3-5 jumle):** Chapter ka overall take — kya achha tha, kaunsa sawaal khula reh gaya, agle chapter ke liye narrator ki theory, viewers se ek sawaal (comment ke liye).
5. **Length balance:** Commentary total script ka lagbhag 25-35% ho. Baqi narration/retelling. Har section mein kam az kam ek jumla narrator ka apna ho.
6. Style wahi: trendy Roman Urdu, dost wali tone, koi bookish lafz nahi.
